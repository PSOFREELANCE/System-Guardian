## 🙏 How Your Support Helps

Your sponsorship directly funds:

- 🖥️ **Development time** — new features, bug fixes, and platform support
- 🔐 **Security audits** — regular third-party reviews
- 🧪 **Test infrastructure** — VMs across Windows 8.1 / 10 / 11
- 📝 **Documentation** — guides, videos, and translations
- 🎨 **Design assets** — icons, illustrations, and branding
- ☁️ **Infrastructure** — CI minutes, code signing certificate

## 💚 Non-Monetary Ways to Support

Not able to sponsor? You can still help enormously:

- ⭐ **Star the repository** — boosts visibility
- 🐛 **Report bugs** — improves the product
- 📝 **Improve documentation** — helps everyone
- 🌍 **Translate the UI** — expands accessibility
- 🗣️ **Share on social media** — spreads the word
- 💬 **Answer questions** in Discussions

## 📜 Sponsor Policy

- Sponsors **never** influence the roadmap, code, or security decisions.
- System Guardian will **always** remain MIT-licensed.
- No sponsor-exclusive features will ever be carved out of the free tier.
- Sponsor logos are reviewed to ensure they align with the project's values.
